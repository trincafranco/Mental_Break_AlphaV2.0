               * Alice:

                  * Keeps the room dim and warm.

                  * Alarm is soft, easy to snooze.

                  * Notifications are heavily suppressed.

                  * If he stirs, she murmurs comforting lines: “You’re okay, the world can wait a bit longer.”

                     * Effects:

                        * Flag morning2_choice = "sleep_in".

                        * Higher chance he misses the quiz outright or shows up late/sluggish.

                        * Short-term Sanity benefit (less acute suffering on wake), long-term academic and guilt cost.

Option M2 – Rouse him loudly to get to class
                           * Alice:

                              * Lights snap to cool daylight.

                              * Alarm + watch/phone buzz aggressively.

                              * She’s talking immediately: “Quiz in 27 minutes. If we skip shower and move, we can still make it.”

                              * (Optional: if you want, fold in “subliminal grindset” for the last few pre-alarm minutes.)

                                 * Effects:

                                    * Flag morning2_choice = "rouse".

                                    * Much higher chance he makes the quiz, but in brittle, exhausted shape.

                                    * Good for “responsible student” narrative and metrics; bad for comfort and possibly sanity.
