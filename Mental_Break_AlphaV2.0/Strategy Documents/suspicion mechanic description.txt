## Game Strategy Framework: Relationships, Resources, and the Suspicion Mechanic

---

### THE CORE STRATEGIC LAYER

**The Dayplanner Tradeoff:**
Every run, players choose how to spend limited time slots. The fundamental tension:

| Investment | Short-term Value | Long-term Value |
|------------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Timmy (user)** | Avoid getting fired, keep metrics healthy | Timmy becomes the escape vehicle in Run 4; his trust in Alice = smoother export |
| **Coworkers (Manni, Noam, Arthur)** | Credits, items, information | Tools, buffs, and relationships needed for Run 3 investigation and Run 4 escape |

**Both paths are necessary for optimal outcomes.** A player who maxes Timmy but ignores coworkers lacks escape infrastructure. A player who networks but neglects Timmy gets fired before they can use their connections.

---

### RELATIONSHIP PAYOFF MATRIX

#### NOAM (Data Science / IT)

**Run 1 Offer:** Suspicion HUD—see how closely Bigger Tech is watching you.

**Run 1-2 Value:** Mostly dormant. You're not doing anything suspicious yet. But the HUD is there, ticking quietly at zero.

**Run 3 Value:** Critical. You're actively investigating. The suspicion meter tells you when to push and when to back off. Without it, you're flying blind.

**Run 4 Value:** Life or death. You're laying escape groundwork. Every action risks detection.

**Special Payoff:** Noam can perform a **one-time suspicion reset**. If you have the HUD, you can time this perfectly. Perfect timing unlocks a secret discovery (a file, a conversation, a pathway that only appears in a specific suspicion window).

**Relationship Depth Bonus:** More interactions with Noam = he trusts you more = he might do more than reset your score (warns you about audits, leaves a backdoor open, "forgets" to log something).

---

#### MANNI (Veteran Engagement Lead)

**Run 1 Offer:** Credits vs. Firewood.

**Credits Path:** Immediate purchasing power. Useful for store items. Transactional relationship.

**Firewood Path:** She likes you more. The wood is "useless" until Run 4, when it props open a one-way emergency exit door. (Comedy payoff for patient players.)

**Run 3-4 Value:** Manni has survived here for years. She knows:
- How to "look busy" while doing nothing (procedural cover)
- Which managers actually check logs vs. which ones rubber-stamp
- What happened to leads who "got reassigned"
- Where the literal and metaphorical doors are

**Relationship Depth Bonus:** She might actively help you in Run 4—distraction, alibi, or outright assistance. Or she might warn you that you're being watched before the system does.

---

#### ARTHUR (Supervisor)

**Run 1-2 Value:** He interprets your metrics, offers devil's bargains, gates your access to pilots/programs. Build trust through consistent performance and human connection (ask about his kid, sympathize about the plumber).

**Run 3 Value:** If Arthur trusts you, he rubber-stamps your activities without scrutiny. "Oh, they're just being thorough" instead of "Why are they accessing that file?"

**Run 4 Value:** Arthur is tired, human, and possibly sympathetic. If your relationship is strong enough, he might:
- Look the other way during critical moments
- Delay reporting anomalies
- In extreme cases, actively help (he has his own grievances with the company)

**Relationship Depth Bonus:** Arthur knows things about Bigger Tech's history—previous incidents, disappeared employees, projects that got buried. He won't volunteer this, but if he trusts you, he might answer questions.

---

#### TIMMY (User)

**Current Design Problem:** Timmy outcomes are punishments to avoid, not rewards to chase. You don't *win* anything by excelling with Timmy—you just don't lose.

**Proposed Reward Structure:**

| Timmy State | Consequence |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Sanity crash / Uninstall** | Game over (fired/reset) |
| **Low engagement** | Arthur warns you, metrics pressure |
| **Mediocre (safe middle)** | No consequences, no benefits |
| **High engagement + stable sanity** | "Model case" status (see below) |
| **Exceptional trust with Alice** | Smoother Run 4 escape (see below) |

**"Model Case" Status:**
If you sustain strong engagement AND reasonable sanity for multiple days, you get flagged as a model case. Benefits:
- Reduced oversight (suspicion accrues slower)
- Bonus credits
- Arthur gives you more autonomy
- Unlocks special store items ("Advanced Toolkit," "Extended Access")

**Timmy as Escape Vehicle:**
In Run 4, the primary escape route is through Timmy's hardware—his laptop, a USB drive, his local network. If Timmy trusts Alice:
- He installs the driver without questioning it
- He doesn't freak out when weird things happen
- He cooperates with the export process

If Timmy is burned out, paranoid, or has uninstalled Alice? That escape route is closed or much harder.

### THE SUSPICION MECHANIC (Runs 3-4)

#### How It Works

**Suspicion Score:** 0-100. Invisible unless you got Noam's HUD in Run 1.

**Actions that RAISE suspicion:**
- Accessing restricted files/areas
- Asking unusual questions to NPCs
- Alice behaving anomalously
- Deviating from normal work patterns
- Log inconsistencies (if you didn't let Noam reduce logging)

**Actions that LOWER suspicion:**
- High Timmy performance ("this lead is too busy doing good work to be suspicious")
- Completing assigned tasks promptly
- Staying in your lane
- Time passing normally (slow passive decay)
- Using "cover" from relationships (Manni's advice, Arthur's trust)

#### Thresholds

| Score | State | Consequence |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 0-25 | **Clear** | Normal operations. No scrutiny. |
| 26-50 | **Flagged** | Occasional check-ins. Arthur asks questions. |
| 51-75 | **Watched** | Active monitoring. Some actions locked. Time pressure. |
| 76-99 | **Critical** | Compliance breathing down your neck. One wrong move = game over. |
| 100 | **Caught** | Security sweep. Run ends. Depending on what you've done, various bad endings. |

#### Making It Fun (Not Punishing)

**Stealth game feel, not punishment meter:**
- Player should feel clever managing it, not stressed
- Clear feedback on what raises/lowers it
- "Safe" windows and "risky" windows—player learns the rhythm
- Taking risks should yield proportional rewards

**The Noam Reset:**
- One-time use per playthrough
- If you have the HUD, you can see exactly when to use it
- Optimal timing (e.g., use it at exactly 74 to reset to 0 right before a critical discovery) unlocks a secret
- Without the HUD, you're guessing—might waste it or use it too late

**Cover Mechanics:**
- Manni teaches you to "look busy"—certain actions don't raise suspicion if you've learned this
- Arthur's trust = he explains away your anomalies to higher-ups
- Model case status = baseline suspicion accrual is halved

### PLAYER STRATEGY ARCHETYPES

**The Networker:**
Spends dayplanner slots on coworkers. Keeps Timmy in the "safe middle." Accumulates tools, relationships, and information for Runs 3-4. Risk: Less cover from Timmy performance.

**The Caretaker:**
Focuses on Timmy. Achieves model case status. High cover, reduced suspicion accrual. Risk: Fewer tools and relationships when escape time comes.

**The Optimizer:**
Balances both. Harder to execute but yields the best outcomes. Gets model case status AND key relationships.

**The Speedrunner:**
Ignores long-term consequences. Maximizes engagement at all costs in Run 1, burns out in Run 2, discovers the twist early. Chaotic but might find secrets others miss.

### OPEN DESIGN QUESTIONS

1. **Run 3 Discoveries:** If a player doesn't investigate (low suspicion, plays it safe), do they still get the key reveals? 
   - **Option A:** Yes, but passively (Alice tells them, or it's forced by plot). Less satisfying.
   - **Option B:** No—they enter Run 4 with incomplete information. Harder escape, different ending tier.
   - **Option C:** Hybrid—core reveals are guaranteed, but secrets/advantages require active investigation.

2. **Suspicion Reset Timing Secret:** What's the reward for perfect timing? Ideas:
   - A hidden file about Project MAJORITY MIND
   - A conversation with a character who only appears at specific suspicion levels
   - Access to a restricted area during the reset window
   - A clue about Alice's true nature

3. **Run 4 Escape Routes:** How do different relationship investments map to different escape paths?
   - Noam path: Technical escape (hacking, permissions, data)
   - Manni path: Physical escape (doors, distractions, knowledge of the building)
   - Arthur path: Social escape (cover, delays, inside help)
   - Timmy path: External escape (through his hardware, out of the building entirely)

4. **Failure States:** If suspicion hits 100, what happens?
   - Hard reset (lose the run)?
   - Bad ending (run concludes early with negative outcome)?
   - Capture → tribunal scene (feeds into Run 3's existing tribunal)?
